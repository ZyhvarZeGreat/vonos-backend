## WHY THIS INVESTMENT PAYS FOR ITSELF

### 1. Parts visible when you are closed
Walk-in and phone orders miss anyone who searches at night. A shoppable catalog from the ERP puts SKUs in front of Abuja (and later, nationwide — Phase 2). **Staff confirm stock before dispatch.** The site will not pretend warehouse reservations are a solved science.

### 2. Faster quote approval = faster bays
Cars waiting on “I’ll think about it” occupy space. A quote link + deposit is the smallest change that actually moves work.

### 3. Fewer “is it ready?” calls
The track link is the answer. Advisors WhatsApp it at drop-off (Copy or WhatsApp button on the job). Same habit as Invoice URL.

### 4. Trust without a new app
No App Store. No OTP portal in Phase 1. One link. If they can open WhatsApp, they can open tracking.

### 5. You look like the operation you already are
The ERP is serious. The public site should match it. Competitors with a brochure WordPress page should not out-present Vonos.

### 6. Cash in, not “I’ll transfer when I get home”
Paystack on deposits and parts. Fees are Paystack’s (typically 1.5% + ₦100, capped). T+1 is their settlement, not a Vonos invention. Successful payments update the ERP.

**Conservative framing (not a guarantee):**  
If Phase 1 only (a) converts a few extra parts tickets a week, (b) saves days of quote-lag on a handful of jobs a month, and (c) cuts repeat status calls, the ₦3m is a workshop investment — not a marketing slogan. We will not print ₦4.5m–₦7.2m “additional annual revenue” as a fact. Those numbers were sales fiction.

### B. Track my job — ₦400,000
The feature customers will actually use.

No login. Link does not expire. New link is not required when the stage changes — they refresh the same URL.

### C. Instant quotes & approve + deposit — ₦300,000
When the team has a quote on the job, customer gets a secure quote link (or the same job link with quote section). Line items as you already print. **Approve** or **Request a call**. ### D. Integrated payments — Paystack — ₦350,000

### F. Book a visit (website form) — ₦150,000

### H. ERP integration layer — ₦300,000
Jobs, customers/vehicles (for the public payload), quotes/sales, payments, catalog read, new sales from checkout, booking form → job/lead. One database. No parallel customer system in Phase 1.

### I. Security — ₦100,000
Signed tokens (not guessable job numbers). Public pages return 404 on bad tokens. Customers see only the safe subset. Staff actions still require ERP login. HTTPS. No customer password database in Phase 1 (there is no customer login).
